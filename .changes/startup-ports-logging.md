---
plan: startup-ports-logging
title: Startup ports, the startup banner and readable terminal logs
level: minor
---

- service: Adds `pickPort` and `PortInUseError` (take the asked port, or fall back to the next free one of 20, with an error naming the port and the config key that sets it), `logStarted` and `StartupBanner` for a single `started` record once a service listens, and the `traceId` and `scope` breadcrumb field names.
- service-logger: `createLogger` now writes readable terminal lines when stdout is a TTY and JSON otherwise, picked outright by the new `format` option or `LOG_FORMAT` (`pretty` is a deprecated alias, and `NODE_ENV` no longer matters); the new `terminal` decorator draws one coloured line per record with the short trace id and scope, an error's cause chain and a startup banner block, while `json` keeps pino's line as is and logged errors keep their cause chain as nested objects; the package now depends on `@open-tomato/service` and no longer on `pino-pretty`.
- service-express: `createService` falls back to the next free port with a warning when its port is taken, rejects with `PortInUseError` under the new `strictPort` (or `PORT_STRICT`), reads `PORT` when the config omits the port, logs a `started` banner with the new `features` config, and returns the bound `port` and `url` on its handle; under `NODE_ENV=test` a port given in config or `PORT` is now honoured; request logs go through the service logger, with an `x-request-id` taken from the request or a new UUID, echoed on the response and logged as `traceId`.
- service-mcp: `createMCP` takes `port`, `strictPort`, `version`, `features` and `health.strictPort` (or `PORT`, `PORT_STRICT`, `HEALTH_PORT` and `HEALTH_PORT_STRICT`), and its HTTP transport and health server fall back to the next free port with a warning instead of failing or crashing, or reject with `PortInUseError` when strict; the handle gains `ready()`, resolving with the bound ports and URLs or rejecting with the start error, `healthUrl()` names the bound health port, a `started` banner is logged once listening, and `McpServer` reports the configured version; a non-integer `PORT` or `HEALTH_PORT` now throws a `RangeError`, `startHealthServer` is async and resolves with its bound port (`HealthServerConfig` is exported), and a failed start no longer exits the process under `NODE_ENV=test` and closes the transport when the health server cannot listen.
- docs: The Express, MCP and logger READMEs document the port and strict settings with their env names, the fallback warning, the `started` banner, the handle's port and URL, `traceId` and `scoped`, and `LOG_FORMAT`.
